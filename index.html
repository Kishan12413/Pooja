The Clinician

Dr. Madan

A physiotherapy practice built on a simple belief: recovery goes further with clinical technique, personalized rehabilitation, and active patient care.

Every treatment plan starts with how you actually move — how you sit, walk, lift, and rest. From there, Dr. Madan crafts targeted physiotherapy interventions tailored specifically to your physical recovery.

  • Assessment-first — no treatment before understanding the root cause.
  • Hands-on therapy — manual technique combined with clinical modalities.
  • Continuous progress — personalized exercises for sustained recovery.

Physiotherapy

Care for how your body moves

Each programme is built around your specific injury, condition, or recovery stage — not a fixed protocol.

Sports Injury Rehab

Structured recovery for sprains, strains, and overuse injuries — built to restore strength and confidence, not just mobility.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided, progressive therapy after orthopedic or joint surgery, paced to how your body is actually healing.

Back & Neck Pain Therapy

Manual therapy and posture correction for chronic pain, disc issues, and the strain of long desk hours.

Electrotherapy & Ultrasound

TENS, ultrasound, and IFT modalities used to reduce inflammation and accelerate tissue repair.

Neuro Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for stroke, Bell's palsy, and neurological conditions affecting movement and coordination.

Geriatric & Joint Care

Gentle, age-appropriate therapy for arthritis, balance, and mobility in older adults.

The body doesn't heal in isolation — the way you move, rest, and recover are all part of one system. Good therapy treats all of it.

— Dr. Madan
